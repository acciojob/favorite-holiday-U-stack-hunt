Paris, France

Paris, the capital of France, is known as the "City of Light" and is famous for its rich history, art, culture, and romantic atmosphere. From iconic landmarks to charming streets and cafés, Paris is a destination that offers something for every traveler.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Eiffel Tower – iconic landmark with panoramic views of the city
  2. Louvre Museum – world-famous museum housing the Mona Lisa
  3. Notre-Dame Cathedral – historic Gothic cathedral
  4. Montmartre – charming neighborhood with artistic vibes
  5. Seine River Cruise – scenic cruise along the beautiful Seine River

Must-Try Foods

  • Croissants – flaky and buttery French pastry
  • Baguette – traditional French bread
  • Macarons – colorful and delicate sweet treats
  • Escargot – classic French snail dish for adventurous eaters
  • Crêpes – thin pancakes with sweet or savory fillings
